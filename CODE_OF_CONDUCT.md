# Code of Conduct

The Agent Name Service (ANS) project follows the
**LF Projects Code of Conduct**:
https://lfprojects.org/policies/code-of-conduct/

It applies to everyone taking part in the project, in every project space:
GitHub repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, mailing lists, chat,
calls, and in-person events, and to conduct outside those spaces when it
affects participation in them.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness behavior that violates the Code of Conduct,
email the Manager of LF Projects at
[manager@lfprojects.org](mailto:manager@lfprojects.org). Do not publish
sensitive reports in a GitHub issue. This reporting route remains available
after the project appoints its own conduct contacts.

The project conduct address is [conduct@ansinfo.ai](mailto:conduct@ansinfo.ai).
Activation is pending; use the LF reporting route above until it is active.

Include what happened, where, when, who was involved, and links to anything
public. Reports are handled confidentially. You will get an acknowledgement
within three business days and be told the outcome.

## Investigation and decision

Until project conduct contacts are appointed, reports follow the LF Projects
Code of Conduct process. The project conduct contacts, once appointed, are
the Community portfolio holder and a second person named in
[TSC.md](TSC.md). They investigate and write a recommendation. If a contact is
conflicted, or the report concerns a TSC member, the Manager of LF Projects
investigates instead. The TSC decides by majority whether there is a finding
and what follows, with the person reported and anyone who shares their
employer recused; if fewer than three members remain, the Manager of LF
Projects decides. The person reported is heard in writing first. The TSC decides in closed
session or by private ballot (GOVERNANCE.md Sections 4.2 and 11). The finding
and the outcome are recorded confidentially, and the reporter is told the
outcome.

## Enforcement

The project may warn, temporarily suspend, or permanently remove anyone from
any role or space. Role removal follows the
[Contributor Ladder](CONTRIBUTOR_LADDER.md); a conduct finding is grounds for
removal from any role under the Ladder's rules for that role. Decisions may be
appealed to the Manager of LF Projects.

## Changes

Every repository's `CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md` points to this file. The TSC may
adopt a project-specific Code of Conduct later; the Charter (Section 4.b)
requires Series Manager approval for that.
