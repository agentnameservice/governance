# Technical Steering Committee

The Technical Steering Committee (TSC) is defined in Section 2 of the
[Charter](CHARTER.md). How it operates is in Section 3 of
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

The voting-member table below also supplies membership of
`@agentnameservice/tsc`. That team has Write access across active project
repositories and CODEOWNERS eligibility for fallback reviews. It may merge in
`governance`; TSC membership alone does not permit merging in other
repositories. The Chair/Vice Chair team controls merges in `.github`.

## Startup Period

The Charter (Section 2.b) names this file as the source of truth for TSC
membership during the Startup Period. The initial TSC takes office on the
Charter adoption date. The Startup Period ends 12 months after adoption or at
the first election, whichever comes first.

- Charter adoption date: November 9, 2026
- Startup Period ends no later than: November 9, 2027
- First election must complete by: November 9, 2027

Seats: five at adoption, seven at the first election, nine at the second
(GOVERNANCE.md Section 3.1). A seat with no eligible candidate stays vacant and does not count
toward any vote.

The initial Chair and Vice Chair elections following Charter adoption have a
one-time waiver of the Maintainer requirement for those officers' initial
terms (GOVERNANCE.md Section 3.3). Both must still be seated TSC members.
Later officer elections, including replacements during the Startup Period,
require the person elected to be a Maintainer.

From the first election of TSC members onward, every TSC candidate must be a
Maintainer or Reviewer. Startup members who want to continue must hold one
of those roles before nominations open. The initial-officer waiver does not
change those candidate requirements.

## Initial voting members

Three of the five members are from one organization. After the first election,
no organization may hold half or more of the seated members, subject to the
holdover exception in Charter 2.d. How many of those three can be re-seated
within the cap depends on how many outside members are seated: two if five
are, three if seven are. Everyone accepting a seat does so knowing that.

holdover needed until a compliant TSC can be seated.

## Chair and portfolios

The Chair and Vice Chair are elected, and the other portfolios are assigned
by the TSC under Sections 3.3 and 3.6 of GOVERNANCE.md. One person may hold
more than one portfolio.

After adoption, the initial TSC will elect its Chair as one of its first
actions, elect the Vice Chair, and assign the Security, Community, and
Standards liaison portfolios, a security backup, and a second conduct
contact. Appointments and their decision records will be listed here.

## Advisors

Non-voting, named by the TSC under Section 3.5 of GOVERNANCE.md. Consulted,
not counted.

The TSC may appoint Advisors after adoption. Appointments will be listed here.

## Election calendar

The first election must be completed by November 9, 2027. After adoption,
the election organizer will announce nomination and voting dates under
Section 3.2 of [GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md).

## Changing this file

Rows in the voting members table change only to record a Section 3 event: a
vacancy filled, an election result, a resignation, or a removal. Each change
links the relevant decision record or resignation. Filling a vacancy during
the Startup Period requires a majority vote of the remaining voting members
(Charter 2.b). Chair elections, resignations, and removals are recorded here
as required by Charter 2.h. Everything else is updated by the Chair as it
happens.
